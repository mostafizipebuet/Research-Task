1.  Project Context 
Imagine that you are on a team that has been asked to design a system that should be able to 
receive an electrical control signal and move the center of mass of the system from an origin 
point to a destination point and back again to the origin point  on board the International 
Space Station (ISS) . The below specifications and requirements have been created by your 
team. 
Your task  is to evaluate the requirements below using the provided ConOps and the 
requirements evaluation form. Please form groups of two and evaluate the requirements to 
the best of your ability using the material you have learned in class so far (see Lecture 14, 
Slides 26 and 31). 
2. Concept of Operations 
The system must be capable of performing two high-level operations:  
1. Displace the center of mass of the system from its origin to a set destination. 
2. Return that point mass back to its origin.

3.  Requirements 
3.1 Functional Requirements 
FR1  Displace: The System shall be able to move a point mass from an origin point to a fixed 
destination point as seen in Figure 1. Note 1: The path shown in the figure is notional; 
the actual path is to be determined. Note 2: Assume that a link between the system 
output and the point mass will be designed separately (by someone else) and will 
match the needs of your design.  Note 3: The origin point is (X, Y, Z) = (0 mm, 0 mm, 
101.6 mm) or [0”, 0”, 4”].  Note 4: The destination point is (X, Y, Z) = (71.84 mm, 0 
mm, -71.84 mm) or [2.82”, 0”, -2.82”]. 
FR2  Return: The System shall be able to move the point mass from the destination point 
back to the origin point.  
3.2 Performance Requirements 
PR1 The system shall execute FR1  accurately with +/ - 2.5mm [0.1”] accuracy in every 
Cartesian direction. 
PR2 The system shall execute FR2 accurately with +/ - 2.5mm [0.1”] accuracy in every 
Cartesian direction. 
PR3  The System shall be able to execute FR1 in no more than 30 seconds. 
PR4 The System shall be able to execute FR1 in no less than 5 seconds.   
PR5  The System shall be able to execute FR2 in no more than 30 seconds.  
PR6  The System shall be able to execute FR2 in no less than 5 seconds.    
PR7  The system shall be able to move a point mass of 3.6  kg perpendicularly to normal 
gravity (9.81 m/s2). 
3.3 Environmental Requirements 
ER1  The System shall operate in an atmosphere comparable to that of Earth. 
Assume 21 degrees centigrade [70 degrees Fahrenheit], with low humidity, 
and pressurized to 100 kPA [750 mm Hg, 14.5 psi].  
ER2  The System shall not contribute any particulates (e.g. dust) to the  ISS 
atmosphere.  
ER3  The System shall enclose all lubricated components to prevent lubricants 
from leaking into the atmosphere of the ISS.  
 
3.4 Resource Requirements  
 RR1  The System shall not use more than 1.5 Watt-hours to support all operations. 
Note: Assume a maximum of 1 minute of operations.
